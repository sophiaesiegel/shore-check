How this works

Official status is read live from the California Department of Public Health (CDPH) advisory map. It's the only thing here that says "don't eat."

Forecast comes from C-HARM (NOAA CoastWatch, UCSC, Scripps). It gives the probability of toxic Pseudo-nitzschia conditions in nearby seawater, on a 3 km grid. It covers domoic acid only, not PSP toxins, and it doesn't measure shellfish directly.

This doesn't apply to commercially sold shellfish from certified dealers. Before harvesting, check CDPH or call 1-800-553-4133.
