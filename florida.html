How this works

Shellfish harvesting status comes from the Florida Department of Agriculture and Consumer Services (FDACS), which opens and closes each harvesting area daily. Closures happen for rainfall, water quality, season or algal toxins; only the toxin closures are about harmful algal blooms.

Red tide samples are Florida Fish and Wildlife (FWC) water samples from the last 8 days, counting Karenia brevis cells.

Satellite layer is MODIS chlorophyll fluorescence (nFLH), the signal often used to spot K. brevis blooms. Other algae and sediment can raise it too, and clouds leave gaps. Always check FDACS and FWC before harvesting.
